# Space budget

Generated by `tools/space_budget.py` from the model in `mechanical/`. Lengths in millimetres.
Part sizes come from `parts/parts.yaml`; several are still unverified there, and the cells
are trade size codes with estimated capacities, not a supplier's drawing.

## Rules the model applies

- The case, its cavity and the board are ellipses with one centre. A part fits when all four of
  its corners are inside the ellipse; a cell fits when its corners, with the gap around it, clear the rim.
- From the chest outwards: back cover, adhesive, cell, gap, board, parts, gap, stone. The stone is flat
  underneath and domed on top, so the height is the one at the centre; the rim is lower by the dome.
- Cell: 8% thickness growth allowed for; 0.2 around it; 0.3 between the swollen cell and the board.
  It lies centred, so that the oval takes the least from its corners.
- The microphones are bottom-port parts: they hang under the board, one at each tip of the oval, with
  their hole through it, 0.9 from the tip. The pads of the cell tabs sit between the cell and microphone B.
- The two microphones are at least 20 apart.
- The strip the cell leaves along one long side holds, under the board, the side-push switch and the
  four spring contacts. The same strip on the other side is the antenna's: no copper there on any layer.
- Every part the case decides has a place on the board and none overlaps another or a keep-out (antenna
  edge, microphone gaskets). The two QFN chips keep 0.3 clear around them.
- The other parts are counted here by area, each with 0.15 of spacing, and may take at most 50% of the
  free board. Their actual places are in the board file; nothing here checks that the tracks can be routed.
